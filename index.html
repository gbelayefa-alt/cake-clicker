How to play Cake Clicker

Goal: Bake as many cakes as possible!

Instructions:

  • Click the big cake to bake cakes.
  • Each click adds cakes based on your click value, which you can see on your scoreboard.
  • Buy upgrades in the Store when you have enough cakes.
  • As you bake more cakes, you can unlock milestones in Rewards.

Upgrades:

  • First click value upgrade that increases click value by one cake. 10 cakes to unlock.
  • Second click value upgrade that increases click value by five cakes. 50 cakes to unlock.
  • Third click value upgrade that increases click value by ten cakes. 100 cakes to unlock.
  • Hiring baker upgrade adds a new baker that automatically clicks every second. 200 cakes to unlock
  • Installing turbo ovens allows every baker to increase the frequency of clicks every time it is purchased. 300 cakes to unlock
